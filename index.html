About

Engineering at the biology–device interface.

My work integrates materials engineering, sensing, electronics and biological validation to create biomedical technologies designed to operate reliably in complex physiological environments.

I am particularly interested in translating optical and electrochemical sensing concepts into integrated systems for infection monitoring, wound care, gastrointestinal health and therapeutic decision-making.
